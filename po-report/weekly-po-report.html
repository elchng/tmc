How to populate this report

This is a reusable template. Feed it one row per purchase order — a table, CSV export, or spreadsheet — using the columns below. The report groups those rows by day, currency and supplier and computes every count and total shown above; nothing here is typed in by hand. USD amounts are converted to Ringgit at the FX rate noted in the footer to produce the RM equivalent and the combined figures.
